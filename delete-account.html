Delete your Setdex account

Deleting your account removes it, and all the training stored in it, from our servers. It cannot be undone.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Setdex and go to Settings (the gear on the Home screen).
  2. Optional: tap Export my data first to keep a copy of your training.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Choose whether the training on this phone stays (without an account) or is removed too.

The account and its data are deleted immediately.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, email contact.plainworklabs@gmail.com from the email address of your account, with the subject Delete my Setdex account. We delete the account and its data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

Deleted: your account (email, and the name and picture Google shared) and all backed-up training — programmes, days, exercises you created, workouts, sets and settings.

Not affected: crash reports (kept up to 90 days) and anonymous statistics, which were never linked to your account (see the privacy policy); and the copy on your own phone if you chose to keep it — uninstalling Setdex removes that.
