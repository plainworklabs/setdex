Privacy policy

Your training is yours

Last updated 3 October 2026

In short. Setdex works fully without an account, and then your training never leaves your phone. With an account, your training is backed up in the EU so you can restore it on another phone. Crash reports carry no personal data. Anonymous usage statistics are sent only if you turn them on. If you use Bring my plan, the screenshots or text you add are read by AI and not kept. We never sell data and never show ads.

Who is responsible

Setdex is made by Samir, trading as Plainwork Labs, based in Israel, who decides how the data described here is used (the "controller"). Contact for anything in this policy: contact.plainworklabs@gmail.com.

What Setdex stores, and why

1. On your phone — always

Your programmes, days, exercises, workouts, sets (weights, reps, times) and settings are stored on your phone. Without an account, this is the only copy: nothing is sent anywhere, except crash reports (section 4), anonymous statistics if you turn them on (section 5), and what you choose to bring with Bring my plan (section 6).

2. Your account — only if you sign in

You can sign in with Google. Google then shares your name, email address and profile picture with Setdex; Setdex never sees your Google password. We use your email and name to identify your account — for example, to see which accounts are active. Your picture is stored by the sign-in service but not used.

3. Backup of your training — only if you sign in

While you are signed in, the training data from section 1 is copied to your account so you can restore it on a new phone and keep several phones in step. It is stored with Supabase in Frankfurt, Germany (EU), sent encrypted, and protected so that each account can only ever read its own rows.

Why we may do this: it is the service you asked for by signing in (performing our agreement with you).

4. Crash reports — always

When the app crashes, a report is sent to Sentry (data stored in Germany, EU) so we can fix the fault. It contains the error and where in the code it happened, the app version, the phone model and Android version, and a random number that identifies the app installation, not you. It contains no name, email, account, IP address, screenshots or training data. Reports are kept for up to 90 days.

Why we may do this: our legitimate interest in keeping the app working, with no personal data involved.

5. Anonymous usage statistics — only if you turn them on

Off until you turn it on (Settings → Data and privacy, or "Yes, share" when the app first asks). If you turn it on, Setdex sends counts and choices to PostHog (EU cloud) such as "a workout was finished, 45 minutes, 18 sets", "a programme was created", "units changed to kg", how the rest timer is set up and the rest times you choose (for example "pull-up: rest 1:30 changed to 2:00"; for an exercise you created, only its muscle group, never its name), when you use Build my plan, the answers you picked (for example "at home, dumbbells, build muscle, some experience, 3 days, 45 minutes"), and, when you use Bring my plan, counts only (for example "3 pictures, read in 20 seconds, 4 days, 24 exercises: 21 matched, 2 to check, 1 new, 2 changed before saving" — never the plan, its exercise names or the pictures), together with the app version, phone model, Android version, language and a random id for this installation. It never sends your name, email, account, the text you type (such as a programme's name), the weights or reps you lift, or your IP address, and it is never linked to your account. While it is off, the app does not contact PostHog at all. Turning it off stops it immediately and replaces the random id.

Why we may do this: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings.

6. Bring my plan — only if you use it

When you use Bring my plan (it needs an account), the screenshots, photos or text you add are sent, encrypted, to our server (Supabase, Frankfurt, EU) and from there to Anthropic (USA), whose AI model Claude reads the training plan in them. Our server keeps them only in memory for that request and never stores them. Anthropic uses them only to provide this service to us, does not use them to train its models, and deletes them within 30 days (longer only if needed to investigate misuse, or by law). Only add what you are happy to have read: crop out names, faces or anything personal that isn't the plan.

For each reading we keep a short record linked to your account — when, how many pictures, whether a plan was found and how much processing it used — to apply the free monthly allowance and to see what the feature costs. It is deleted when you delete your account, and after 12 months at the latest.

If you have also turned on anonymous statistics (section 5), the names of exercises in your plan that Setdex doesn't have yet (for example "Sled Push") are counted on our server — just the name and a count, not linked to you or your account — so we can add the most wanted ones.

Why we may do this: it is the service you asked for by using Bring my plan (performing our agreement with you); counting exercise names relies on your consent to statistics.

How long data is kept

  • Account and backed-up training: until you delete your account. Deleting it removes the account and all its training from our servers immediately.
  • On your phone: until you remove it in the app or uninstall Setdex.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • Bring my plan: the screenshots and text are not kept by us; Anthropic deletes them within 30 days. The record of each reading: until you delete your account, at most 12 months.
  • Anonymous statistics: kept to see trends over time; they cannot be linked back to you, so they cannot be found or deleted per person.

Your rights

You can at any time:

  • Get a copy of all your training: Settings → Data and privacy → Export my data (a JSON file other apps and spreadsheets can read).
  • Correct anything in the app itself.
  • Delete your account and everything in it: Settings → Account → Delete account, or see Delete your account.
  • Withdraw consent for statistics: Settings → Data and privacy.
  • Ask what we hold about you, or object to or restrict its use, by writing to contact.plainworklabs@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.
  • Complain to a data-protection authority: in Israel, the Privacy Protection Authority; in the EU, the authority of the country where you live.

Security

Everything sent between the app and our services is encrypted (HTTPS). On the server, rules in the database itself make sure an account can only read and change its own data.

Children

Setdex is not meant for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes, and important changes are shown in the app before they take effect.
